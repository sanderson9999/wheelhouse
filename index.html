Wheelhouse

SALT LAKE CITY

Wheelhouse is an infoshop and radical community space in downtown Salt Lake City. We offer a lending library of 800+ radical books, local artist shop and gallery, bookstore, and reservable event space! Join us for film screenings, talks, organizing, and more.
